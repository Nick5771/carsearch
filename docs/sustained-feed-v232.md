# Sustained recommendation fixes v232

The real v231 test showed seven Star Wars products among the opening ten screenshots but only fifteen among forty-eight overall. Later replenishment drifted toward laptops, Harry Potter and generic exploration. These corrections remain within the v230 catalogue treatment and isolated test cohort; no measured commercial outcome or Meta-level quality is claimed.

## Sustained interest and retrieval

The focus decision now consults distinct deliberate likes/saves/relevance feedback in the account-scoped journal and current saved list. Neutral impressions cannot erase those deliberate signals when the topic's older forty-product summary fills up. Recent genuine negative evidence, including quick skips and explicit rejection, can weaken focus. Gift families and gift missions are excluded. Evidence reads are cached against exact local journal/saved content and expire by minute; account switches clear the cache.

When a focused candidate pool exists, rolling category/brand penalties no longer grow enough to overwhelm specific interest. Exploration repetition is measured separately from the preferred theme; a soft rolling density target and bounded runs preserve variety. The policy does not guarantee a fixed category share when relevant inventory is absent or the person's interests change.

Focus lanes retain the theme when trying query variants, rather than widening into generic LEGO. Each replenishment can make at most two extra rounds of up to two focused lanes before broad discovery, using existing request budgets and stale-feed guards. Catalogue paging allows bounded later provider-page checks, and background refresh uses the requested page rather than repeatedly refreshing page one. Provider/inventory exhaustion remains finite.

Two distinct deliberate positive products can support Hoth, walkers or collector-set subqueries and a bounded ranking boost. These are derived from actual stored product titles/evidence, not hardcoded preferences for this user. Collector detection uses explicit UCS wording and a small known-set identifier list; it is a limited taxonomy, not general visual understanding or authenticity verification. Existing and future classifiers on client/server agree.

## Product quality and guest likes

Cooling pads, laptop coolers, screen protectors, keyboard covers, charging cables and lens caps are classified as accessories. Complete-product recommendation lanes exclude them. Untested/nonworking/parts listings are excluded unless the requested query explicitly signals repair/parts intent. This does not prove a seller's description or product condition.

Signed-out Profile shows a heart tab and 'Liked on this device' grid using the existing product tile/opening UI. It reads browser-local likes and does not make an authenticated saved-products request. Saved-product storage now participates in the existing account archive so guest likes cannot silently become another account's evidence. Guest DOM contents and inferred sub-interest caches clear at account changes. No automatic guest-to-account merge is introduced.

## Validation

The full-app regression selects six successive ten-card batches from adequate mixed inventory and requires at least six theme matches in every batch, limits repeated laptop exploration and verifies sixty unique product identities. It separately checks that sixty neutral exposures preserve deliberate focus, fresh rejection weakens it, Hoth/UCS queries need distinct real positive fixtures, query variants retain theme, cooling pads and repair-intent constraints work, and guest likes remain isolated after login. Fixture results are implementation tests, not production relevance measurements.

The continuous-discovery fixture waits for the asynchronous pagination outcome within a bounded second instead of assuming completion at 160 ms. Recommendation, shopping sync, catalogue, intelligence, motion and account regressions accompany the change. Live catalogue probes verify current retrieval/filtering; the user must still retest sustained real-feed relevance. StockX image delivery is unchanged.
