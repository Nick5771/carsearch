# Responsive recommendation fixes v231

This fixes the v230 catalogue treatment after a real test returned a stale Home opening, broad LEGO recommendations, photographed booster-box accessories, repeated liked products and a short feed. The existing forced test link and v230 experiment remain; this is a corrective release, not a claimed improvement in measured retention or accuracy.

Meaningful searches, likes, saves and explicit relevance feedback invalidate the Home candidate cache and exhausted lane state. A debounced refresh prepares up to three lanes in the background without changing Search or hidden Home. Returning to Home applies the prepared pool at the current position; engagement while already watching Home preserves the active card and refreshes the tail. Motion, scroll, account, cancellation and stale-revision guards prevent inappropriate replacement. Provider failures keep the existing feed usable. Fresh lane progress is persisted after application.

A recent specific search supplies temporary focus. At least three distinct positive products supporting that specific topic promote it to strong focus; gift context cannot promote durable personal focus. Theme matches receive bounded ranking/sequencing priority. Strong focus permits up to five consecutive matching cards while other candidates are available. Exploration remains, family repeats are penalized, and a pool with only relevant unique candidates can continue without a category cap terminating it. This is explainable policy, not a trained user-interest model.

Saved products and known matching variants supply evidence instead of being inserted as reminders. The older three-result Search injection is disabled in the new treatment so it cannot put saved products back into the refreshed queue. Home drains eligible backlog before requesting more inventory and prioritizes focus lanes during replenishment. Catalogue paging reserves bounded opportunities to consult subsequent provider pages instead of treating a small indexed window as the whole inventory. Finite inventory, unavailable providers and network limits can still end a feed; results are not fabricated.

Title classification treats display cases, acrylic cases and box/protector cases as accessories, even when a booster box is pictured. These are excluded from general product lanes but explicit accessory searches remain supported. Server and client classifiers match. AI cannot override a known accessory into a complete product. Title rules do not certify a seller's actual contents or authenticity.

Qualified reel attention requires a loaded visible image. A card without an image element is no longer considered visually readable, preventing blank cards from generating negative skip evidence. This does not repair StockX image delivery; that remains secondary provider/image work.

Validation includes a full-app Search-to-Home test, theme-dominant sequencing, saved-product exclusion, legacy injection protection, active-card preservation, sparse backlog drainage, missing/loaded image attention and late-account response guards. Recommendation, shopping sync, catalogue, intelligence, continuous-discovery and motion regressions are checked. These tests support implementation correctness; real-user relevance still requires retesting.
