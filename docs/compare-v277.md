Compare has its own source-item context; ordinary typed Search keeps its existing ranking. The camera button is shared visually by both Search screens and opens the existing camera flow.

The summary contains only Lowest, Typical price and Matching listings. Typical is the median of at least two loaded matching offers with known shipping. Lowest is the minimum known delivered total. Unknown shipping remains null, is labelled on the card, sorts after known totals and is excluded from price statistics. Currency is fixed to the source item; there is no implied currency conversion. Matching listing count includes the loaded matches with unknown shipping. The information control explains that these are current asking prices among loaded matches, not confirmed sale prices or the whole market.

Matching requires the source/query identity tokens, rejects incompatible variant markers and accessory/empty-box offers, and retains the source's condition group when known. These conservative lexical rules can miss valid differently worded listings; they do not authenticate items. Broad Sailor Moon queries are refined with source-character tokens, with tests for Chibi Moon and Pluto mismatches.

Compare requests ascending price order from the shopping engine and eBay provider. The shared server window cache separates sort mode and comparison currency. Known delivered totals are sorted before pagination; the client applies stricter identity checks, deduplicates and re-sorts on subsequent batches. Sparse initial batches can fetch up to two further pages, and Load more remains available if no matches survived the first batch. Existing request ownership guards protect against stale searches and pagination responses.
